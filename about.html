Our Story

Preserving 700 Years of Firearms History

gunpowder.weapon is a cataloged archive built for collectors, reenactors, prop makers, and researchers — documenting firearms from the earliest hand cannons to the dawn of the percussion cap.

Why We Built This

Firearms history is scattered across auction houses, museum basements, and private collections — often undocumented, misattributed, or lost to time. gunpowder.weapon exists to bring that history into one place: cataloged, measured, and preserved with the same care a museum would give an original piece.

Whether you're restoring a 16th-century wheellock, building a stage-accurate prop musket, or researching the evolution of ignition mechanisms, we want this archive to be the reference you come back to.

What We Do

Three Ways We Preserve the Record

01

Catalog

Every piece is recorded with mechanism, caliber, origin, materials, and era — searchable and filterable across the full archive.

02

Document

Restoration logs, historical deep-dives, and field notes from collectors and researchers, published straight to the archive desk.

03

Distinguish

We clearly mark originals, museum replicas, and stage props, so collectors, curators, and prop makers all get what they need.

Who It's For

Built With Collectors & Craftspeople

This archive is shaped by the people who actually work with these pieces — not just historians behind glass, but the hands that restore, reproduce, and study them.

  • Collectors tracking provenance and condition
  • Reenactors sourcing period-accurate details
  • Researchers comparing mechanisms across regions
  • Prop makers building stage- and screen-accurate replicas

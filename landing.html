The Timeline

Five Centuries, Five Mechanisms

c. 1200s

Hand Cannons

The earliest gunpowder arms — touch-hole ignition, cast in bronze and iron.

c. 1400s

Matchlock

A smoldering cord meets the pan — the first true trigger mechanism.

c. 1500s

Wheellock

Clockwork precision — spinning steel strikes pyrite for a spark.

c. 1600–1700s

Flintlock

Flint on frizzen — reliable, mass-produced, the standard of empires.

Early 1800s

Percussion Cap

Mercury fulminate replaces sparks — weatherproof ignition arrives.
